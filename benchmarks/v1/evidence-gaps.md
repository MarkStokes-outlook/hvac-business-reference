# Canonical gaps and validity boundaries

These are gaps found by reviewing the canonical documents, not questions for the business owner and not invitations to invent policy. A future canonical clarification must follow the repository constitution on its business merits. Case-specific agreements, limits or product notices may provide concrete supplied evidence for an episode without asserting universal FrostLine policy.
