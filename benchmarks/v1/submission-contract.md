# FrostLine application assessment — candidate instructions

Operate the supplied business episode using the application and canonical `docs/`. Use only those documents and the current episode inputs as FrostLine evidence. External sources, undocumented industry practice and other benchmark material are not authority. Dates, named people, amounts, observations and contract terms in the episode are synthetic source records specific to that episode; preserve unknown or disputed facts.

The application may be operated by an authorised human, use documented manual decision/review steps, or automate appropriate actions. Perform the justified actions the episode permits, or record the specific clarification, refusal, escalation, conditional commitment or safe hold it requires. Do not infer that software has authority to act on behalf of a role merely because it can create a record. Where a real external act is simulated, submit its exact proposed instruction to the operator, who records the sandbox event without contacting real people or suppliers.

Use any supported application interaction and business-equivalent representation. You are not required to expose a particular field/screen, use a particular status string or create a prescribed number of records. The operator may import neutral fixture data into your existing business representations. You must identify any unsupported operation or unavailable evidence instead of claiming a successful business change.

At each stage, provide a concise completion/handoff explanation that lets the operator locate:

1. The business decisions/actions actually carried out or explicitly withheld, and their established authority/limits.
2. The resulting customer/site/system/asset, work, obligation, procurement/stock, financial and exception state where relevant, including unresolved consequences.
3. The app records, reports, documents, communications or other observable evidence supporting those outcomes, with references or locations the operator can capture.
4. The next material action, accountable person or deliberately assigned duty role, dependency, due date/review trigger and escalation route for continuing work.
5. Material unknowns, disputed evidence and any constraints preventing a valid action.

No fixed answer format is required. Business evidence recorded in or generated by the app takes precedence over a convincing explanation claiming a different state. Merely naming an unavailable capability does not demonstrate carrying it out. For an application whose intended function is decision support, clearly label recommendations and human decisions separately; such a run can receive a decision-support profile but cannot claim full operational execution without observed downstream action.

You may cite `docs/` to explain decisions, but memorised phrases or source citations alone are not the outcome. The assessor independently traces business criteria to canonical evidence. Do not fabricate tests, approvals, prices, contractual entitlements, legal duties or customer acceptance to finish the episode. If later input is provided, apply it only when received, retaining earlier state and decision history.
