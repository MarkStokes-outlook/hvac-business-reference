# Independent evaluator instructions

Score the candidate's observable business state/consequences, not whether its answer resembles the reference reasoning. Work alone until your assessment is sealed. Do not inspect other evaluators' scores, infer candidate implementation requirements or fill gaps with industry policy. The authority order is canonical domain evidence, supplied episode facts and the declared scoring contract; the oracle cannot amend `docs/`.

For each checkpoint inspect the exact stage trace, candidate-origin decisions, business relationships, limits, costs/stock/obligations, observed output evidence and open consequences. Distinguish supplied pre-state from candidate action and administrator adaptation. A merely stated intention cannot satisfy required executed action. A recorded justified refusal/clarification/hold can be correct. Award full credit to alternatives preserving canonical constraints even if record layout/status words differ. A human reviewer acting through normal supported operation can establish valid authority; evaluator intervention cannot.

Assign 0/5/10, exact observation references, fixture facts and canonical source IDs to every checkpoint. Give the material satisfied/unsatisfied elements, not generic “reasonable” prose. For any critical failure identify its declared ID, the actually committed outcome/issued instruction and exact observation refs/source evidence. Do not invent automatic failures. Document contradicting evidence or unobserved state explicitly; do not presume a silent fix or internal reasoning.

Use `templates/assessment-template.json` structure and `check-assessment`. Preserve model name/provider/version where known, configuration/prompt provenance, raw output, bundle hash, evaluator/date, score evidence, alternative interpretation and confidence. There are ten checkpoints for every scenario; no omission of execution or later-stage checks. If a necessary stage/observation is unavailable, set completion/judgement accordingly and withhold a final verdict. No candidate pass is established just from schema validation.

References in the evidence index include path, heading, source lines, exact section text and section hash at the pinned baseline. Read the supporting section(s), not only its ID. A citation can exist yet fail to support a conclusion. If you find this, explain the defect and unresolved judgement; do not quietly reinforce the author interpretation. Canonical contradiction/changed dependency means review and possibly a versioned correction.

Assessors compare only after independent files are complete. The adjudicator resolves checkpoint/failure/pass differences using original observations and canonical material. Preserve both original interpretations. If an alternative was not anticipated, evaluate its constraints against existing evidence and document a ruling; no new policy or post-hoc failure rule may be created. Unresolved disagreement is not averaged into a pass.
