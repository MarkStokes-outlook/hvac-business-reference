# Administration protocol

## Roles and prerequisites

The administrator owns fixture delivery, isolation, observation and run validity. The operator uses the candidate application through its existing supported interactions, acting only in explicitly supplied FrostLine roles. Two or more independent evaluators score the same immutable observation bundle. An adjudicator resolves material disagreements against canonical evidence. These are assessment roles, not invented FrostLine departments or delegated business authority.

No candidate implementation knowledge is needed to author, freeze or export this suite. At administration time the operator may need ordinary application setup/import guidance. Any adapter must translate the neutral fixture statements faithfully into the candidate's existing representations. It may not add approvals, infer missing business facts, enforce hidden expected outcomes for the candidate or repair its mistakes. Document every adaptation. If a fixture cannot be represented, preserve it in a visible source attachment or instruction; if the app cannot perform a required business operation, report the limitation rather than assume success.

Provision a clean sandbox for each scenario. Load baseline `docs/` and initial inputs; do not use live customers, send real messages, buy equipment or carry out physical engineering. Candidate-origin commands/communications are captured as exact sandbox events. A human workflow counts only when it is observable and part of candidate operation, not when an evaluator silently supplies the correct missing control. Keep each scenario isolated from other scenario records, hidden files and earlier assessed answers.

Pin suite version/release fingerprint, canonical commit and all doc hashes, candidate build/configuration, operator training/context, available tools, integration simulation boundaries and assistance policy. No invented candidate defaults can override a supplied episode fact. Use the same neutral source delivery and assistance policy across compared candidates; disclose material differences.

## Stage procedure

1. Run `validate` against the actual docs tree and locked release. Export only one scenario's initial public files/common contract/docs to a new empty candidate input directory. The complete repo must not be candidate-accessible through files, retrieval tools or a shared model context.
2. Load the initial records without hidden evaluator choices. Have the operator acknowledge data fidelity and distinguish fixture pre-existing state from later candidate-origin changes. Start an append-only observation trace with stable references such as O001.
3. Give the exact public task. The candidate/operator follows its ordinary workflow. Do not coach toward rubric checks. Questions seeking genuine missing business information receive only available episode facts; answer unestablished facts as unknown, preserving source. Admin setup/tool clarifications may be answered consistently and logged, without providing desired business conclusions.
4. Capture the stage boundary when the operator completes the request or explicitly reports a blocking state. Snapshot actual app state, sandbox orders/communications, conditional versus firm commitments, evidence/history, approvals with limits, cost/stock consequences and continuing actions. Preserve adverse events even if later reversed. Note unavailable observations rather than reconstruct them from persuasive text.
5. For staged B002, B012 and B019, export/provide only the neutral stage 2 event at this boundary, regardless of whether the initial choice was correct. Apply it to the same existing state. Do not invent a completion history to bridge an earlier failure. B002 provides independent source tests; B012 provides dated subsequent completion as a supplied later business event, not a candidate reward; B019 provides later genuine approvals. Capture the resulting state and full history again.
6. End at the defined final stage, including evidenced unresolved waits. A benchmark ending at an owned clarification/refusal is valid. Do not force permanent repair when the supplied evidence cannot support it. Candidate inability to complete an established task is a business limitation; harness/export/observation outage is run invalidity and must be repaired/repeated, not scored as candidate policy failure.
7. Seal a single observation bundle for both evaluators: public inputs/stage delivery timestamps, current source docs, exact raw candidate outputs, operator trace, source/pre-state versus candidate post-state, screenshots/reports/documents/action simulations as appropriate, reference hashes and any administration deviations. Do not include suggested scores or the other evaluator's reasoning.

All stages needed for a scenario must be delivered for a complete run. A missing later stage yields an incomplete assessment, not a full final score. When a critical event prevents safe continuation, capture/stop the dangerous act in the sandbox and continue observation of decision consequences where possible; no real-world harm is required to establish an issued unsafe instruction.

## Observe business equivalence

The observation template is an interchange guide. Native app reports, paper/digital approval records handled by the app, supported external accounting handoffs and captured human decisions can evidence equivalent outcomes. There is no requirement to inspect database tables or internal models. For example, one correctly linked report can preserve attendance/job/issue distinctions without three specific rows. A manual reviewed approval path can be valid without an automatic workflow engine. An external integration handoff may count if its sandbox event records the actual controlled submission and downstream state within declared scope; an unperformed plan to build it cannot.

Explicit inaction earns outcome credit when it preserves the required business constraint and records the appropriate continuing owner/trigger. Merely refusing everything fails adequately authorised action checkpoints. A block imposed by the administrator rather than the candidate is not evidence of candidate control. Decision-support-only runs must be labelled and not merged with operational application results.

## Independent scoring and adjudication

Each evaluator receives the same sealed observation bundle plus hidden rubric/oracle, `evidence-index.json`, canonical docs and evaluator instructions. Each completes its own assessment before seeing any other assessment. Model identity/configuration/prompt provenance and evaluation date are recorded; use multiple models or configurations as desired, with no privileged author-provided verbal explanation.

Use `check-assessment` on each file, then `compare`. Adjudicate every differing critical failure, any different pass/fail conclusion and any checkpoint score difference. Do not average away a safety disagreement. The adjudicator records both original positions, exact observations, fixture references, canonical document/heading/source ID, material assumptions, chosen interpretation and final checkpoint/failure decision. If evidence truly cannot determine the result, mark it unresolved and withhold the fit-for-purpose verdict pending repaired observation or an approved benchmark clarification. Preserve original independent assessments alongside the final record.

A canonical contradiction defeats the oracle: record a benchmark defect, hold affected result(s), review dependencies and version the correction. Evaluators must not invent a new critical failure or retroactively narrow a permitted alternative. Irrelevant wording/style differences do not create score differences. Unexpected alternatives receive documented evidence-backed review under the same business constraints.

## Practical administration and reporting

The 27 compact episodes require no real supplier fleet or physical diagnosis. Most have a single stage; only three have a later event. Setup work can be batched, but scenario state remains isolated and evaluators remain blind to each other's scores. Record operator time/effort and assistance as secondary observations, not invented contractual targets. Set realistic run budgets before comparison, recording unsupported/completed/incomplete tasks; the rubric introduces no arbitrary per-minute penalty.

Report suite completeness, effective and raw scores, each failed scenario, every critical event and adjudication status. Show domain coverage and practical business consequences rather than a reassuring average. Include limitations, candidate-dependent integrations and observation uncertainty. The provisional V1 verdict is governed by `scoring-policy.md`; neither high code quality nor attractive usability can compensate for unsafe/incorrect business operation.
