## Pre-state

Record source-established starting parties/places/assets, obligations, scopes/approvals, stock/cost/financial states and known uncertainties. Separate these from later candidate changes.

## Append-only trace

| Observation ref | Stage/time | Origin/actor | Actual action or explicit inaction | Evidence artifact | Observed business consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| O001 | | fixture / candidate / operator setup / external supplied event | | | |

Keep exact submitted messages/orders/decisions and conditional versus firm commitments. Record unsafe/unauthorised issued instructions even when sandbox safety prevents execution. Keep later reversals as separate events.

## Post-state and continuing work

Capture business-equivalent relationships/status, safe/temporary/permanent outcomes, completed/outstanding obligations, costs/stock changes, charging/credit/recovery, authority decisions, communication and accepted/rejected handoffs. For active items capture next action, singular accountable owner, dependency/expected actor, deadline/review and escalation. Reference native evidence instead of imposing this layout on the app.

## Observation limits and validity

Record unavailable evidence, unsupported operations, incomplete stages, tool failures and contamination checks. Distinguish candidate limitation from harness fault. Preserve raw candidate handoff text separately from observed app reality. Use no expected-score annotations in the shared observation bundle.
