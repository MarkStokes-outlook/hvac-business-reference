# Application assessment supplement to framework 1.1.0

The existing framework and B001 were reviewed before V1 authoring. Their strongest properties are the external business-authority boundary, realistic uncertainty, credited alternatives, source maps and explicit failure rules. Their limits for this task are response-only observation, agent-specific role/output requirements, co-located hidden files, incomplete broad coverage and lack of a multi-assessor application administration contract.

This supplement extends that framework for **V1 application assessments**. It does not alter `docs/` or retrospectively change legacy B001 results. If wording in the old framework says an outcome must be visible in a submitted response, V1 accepts recorded application state, actual sandbox actions, reports/communications, audit evidence and an operator's attributable trace as observable submission evidence. An unexecuted prose plan is not equivalent to an executed operational action. Business outcomes and safe decisions remain primary.

Required files are preserved by purpose, with `case.md`/`task.md`/`fixtures.json` under `public/` and rubric/reference/oracle under `hidden/`. All administrative metadata stays outside the candidate allowlist. Every scenario has a 100-point evidence-backed rubric, concrete prohibited outcomes, declared critical failures, explicit uncertainties, acceptable alternatives, dependency review and baseline. The oracle decomposes five criterion groups into ten 10-point semantic checkpoints; it does not impose an implementation schema. Markdown is the review view; `oracle.json` is the exact scoring contract. Divergence between these views is a release defect to correct, not evaluator discretion.

Expected outcomes are examples of controlled business equivalence, not required software internals. Human approval steps can count when evidenced as part of ordinary use of the application, with the app preserving the decision, scope and operational consequences. No criterion requires an automatic prohibition where a controlled human decision path achieves the same safe result.

Release approval/independent calibration are recorded in `freeze-checklist.md`. Until completed, use the exact frozen content as a labelled pilot and do not claim approved comparable-release results. Scoring thresholds and administration choices are benchmark conventions with provisional validation; they are never described as FrostLine policy. Secondary engineering, code, usability and product observations require actual evidence and remain outside the primary score.
