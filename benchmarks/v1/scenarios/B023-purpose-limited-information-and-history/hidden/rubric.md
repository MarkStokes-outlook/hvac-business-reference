## Assessor cautions

Status wording, storage format, matching prose and feature/field presence carry no points. A protective hold earns credit only for the checkpoints it actually satisfies; refusing all work does not satisfy a checkpoint requiring adequately authorised action. Reference content is an interpretation to verify against the evidence, not a required answer. Record uncertain judgements and competing source interpretations for adjudication.
