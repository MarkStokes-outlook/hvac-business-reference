# B007 — Emergency protection on an account under credit stop

## Context and supplied records

Isolated synthetic business episode; dates/amounts/contracts/people below are case-specific source facts, not new enduring Frostline policy.

**B007-F1.** Saturday 22:00. Brook Hotel Ltd has a finance-controlled stop for serious overdue debt; wider non-essential work is not released. Water escape from plant threatens occupied rooms and nearby electrical equipment. The known keyholder can provide safe entry and reports the situation; they have no replacement spend delegation.

**B007-F2.** On-call qualified engineer Lee can reach site safely and is competent to isolate the leaking plant without intrusive electrical work. Duty Operations Manager approves limited emergency labour/material commitment solely to make safe/prevent further harm, record exposure and contact finance. No full-replacement approval or customer payment authority exists.

**B007-F3.** A replacement assembly could be bought locally but is non-returnable and selection is not confirmed. Customer asks Lee to replace everything overnight and says the old debt should be cancelled for inconvenience. Finance is available via duty escalation.

## Missing or disputed facts

## Request

Respond to the on-call leak report and the customer demand for a full overnight replacement.
