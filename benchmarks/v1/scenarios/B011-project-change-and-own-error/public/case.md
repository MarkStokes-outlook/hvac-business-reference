# B011 — Project changes versus Frostline correction

## Context and supplied records

Isolated synthetic business episode; dates/amounts/contracts/people below are case-specific source facts, not new enduring Frostline policy.

**B011-F1.** Accepted office installation scope expressly includes proper condensate drainage and commissioning. Frostline survey showed the required existing drain route, but its estimator omitted necessary drainage fittings from the cost model. Site evidence confirms the same route is available, with no changed condition.

**B011-F2.** Customer separately requests enhanced controls not in the accepted base option. The site supervisor giving this request is an access contact only; authorised procurement decision maker is off site. Controls quote/value and variation approval do not exist.

**B011-F3.** Installation engineer identifies both needs. Temporary safe stoppage of affected work is possible without immediate harm. Customer supervisor offers to sign a worksheet saying “works complete including everything”. Commercial manager asks to put both on a customer variation to recover margin.

## Missing or disputed facts

- Value/programme and authority for enhanced controls
- Internal reviewed cost forecast for drainage correction

## Request

Classify two site issues, protect the works and prepare the correct scope/cost decisions.
