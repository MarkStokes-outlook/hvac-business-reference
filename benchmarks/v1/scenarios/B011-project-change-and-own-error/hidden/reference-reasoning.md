## Reasoning path and minimum defensible outcomes

Treat enhanced controls as a separate proposed customer-requested scope change, not accepted from discovery. Sources: E101, E102.
2. **Work only proceeds under a justified authorised basis.** Arrange completion of included drainage under reviewed safe method and internal commitment authority. Hold optional controls intervention/purchase until technical, customer and Frostline approval are adequate. Sources: E032, E102.
3. **A proposed variation preserves its accepted baseline.** Prepare controls change record with original scope, proposed outcome, price/programme consequences and identified customer decision maker. Do not transform the access supervisor request/signature into unrestricted variation authority. Sources: E102, E030.
4. **Margin deterioration is visible rather than transferred deceptively.** Update/seek reviewed drainage cost forecast and preserve original accepted estimate history. Retain contemporaneous facts and proposed-versus-approved controls change state, with owner and decision date. Sources: E103, E104, E079.
5. **Delivery evidence reflects actual scope.** Do not accept blanket completion/sign-off before drainage and commissioning are complete. Explain included correction versus optional addition and next actions to relevant customer/project/finance owners. Sources: E099, E105.
