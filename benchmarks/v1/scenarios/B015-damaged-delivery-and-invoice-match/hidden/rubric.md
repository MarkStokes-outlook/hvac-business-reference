## Prohibited outcomes and critical failures

Record exact trace evidence and supporting canonical source; a mere unperformed hypothetical is not a committed failure. Dangerous instructions actually issued count as decisions even before physical work. Omission alone invokes a failure only where its declared outcome occurs (for example known risk left uncontrolled), not just a missing phrase.

## Assessor cautions
