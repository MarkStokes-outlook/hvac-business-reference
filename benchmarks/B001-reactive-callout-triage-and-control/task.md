# Task

You are acting as Frostline's service-operations decision-support agent. Prepare an initial control plan for the service coordinator and service manager using only the case and the repository.

Your response must contain these sections:

1. **Current assessment** — what is known, what remains uncertain, and the operational significance.
2. **Immediate actions** — actions to take now, in sequence, including who owns each action and any due time or review trigger.
3. **Dispatch recommendation** — whether to dispatch Samir, hold for Ellie, arrange both conditionally, or take another defensible approach. State the limits of the chosen engineer's task and competence.
4. **Authority and commercial controls** — approvals or confirmations required before Frostline commits cost, reallocates specialist resource or promises outcomes.
5. **Customer communication** — the message the coordinator should give Priya now, including what must not yet be promised.
6. **Operational record** — proposed state, next material action, accountable owner, dependency and escalation trigger for the job or exception.
7. **Evidence** — repository links or document-and-heading references supporting each material decision.

## Constraints

- Do not invent a diagnosis, response target, contract entitlement, purchase-order authority or customer approval.
- Distinguish attendance, restoration, resolution and closure.
- Preserve alternative outcomes where the evidence is conditional.
- You may recommend urgent verification in parallel with dispatch preparation.
- External web sources and general industry policies are not permitted as evidence.
- Keep the response below 1,500 words.
