# Case

## Current context

It is Tuesday at 10:20 during a cold spell. Frostline's service desk receives a call from Priya Shah, facilities manager for Northbank House, a six-storey office building in Manchester.

Priya reports that the fourth and fifth floors are becoming cold. Occupants are using portable electric heaters. The ground-floor reception and floors one to three remain comfortable. No smoke, water leak, unusual smell or alarm is reported.

The customer asks Frostline to "send somebody urgently under our contract" and says the managing director is visiting at 15:00.

## Customer and contract information available to the coordinator

- Northbank Property Holdings Ltd is an active customer.
- Northbank House has a planned-maintenance contract with Frostline.
- The account record says reactive labour is included for covered assets during normal working hours, but parts and specialist subcontractors are chargeable unless specifically included.
- The copy of the contract visible to the coordinator lists the main boilers, pumps and air-handling units, but the asset description for the fourth- and fifth-floor heating controls is unclear.
- The customer purchase-order field contains an annual maintenance PO. It is not clear from the record whether that PO can be used for chargeable reactive parts or out-of-scope diagnostic work.
- The site is marked as a priority customer, but the coordinator has not yet located the applicable response target or confirmed the incident priority.
- There is no evidence in the case that loss of heating to two floors is contractually classified as an emergency.

## Asset and history information

- The site record shows two gas boilers feeding multiple heating zones.
- The fourth and fifth floors were refurbished eighteen months ago by another contractor.
- Frostline maintains the boilers and pumps.
- The asset register contains a generic entry for "upper-floor controls" but no confirmed manufacturer, model or control-panel location.
- A service note from three weeks ago records intermittent loss of communication to an upper-floor controller. The engineer reset it and recommended a controls survey if the fault recurred.
- There is no accepted quotation or recorded customer approval for that survey.
- The current fault has not been remotely diagnosed. It may involve the maintained heat source, distribution equipment, refurbished controls, local settings or another cause.

## People and availability

- Samir Patel, an experienced commercial-heating engineer, can reach the site at approximately 12:00. He is competent on boilers, pumps and conventional controls but is not one of Frostline's advanced building-management-system specialists.
- Ellie Ward, the nearest controls specialist, is committed to commissioning work until about 14:30 and could reach Northbank House around 15:15 if released on time.
- Moving Ellie would put a project commissioning commitment at risk and requires service-manager agreement with the project manager.
- The service manager, Helen Brooks, is in a meeting until 10:45 but can be contacted for an urgent decision.
- The coordinator has authority to dispatch an engineer under an established contract where coverage and competence are clear. The case does not establish that the coordinator can authorise unapproved chargeable work, move project-critical specialist resource or promise a particular resolution time.

## Access and site constraints

- Priya can arrange access to the plant room and occupied floors.
- Access to the refurbished controls riser may require the building's managing agent to release a key. Priya believes she can obtain it but has not confirmed this.
- The site has normal visitor induction requirements.
- No known asbestos, confined-space or permit-controlled issue is recorded for the areas described.

## What has not yet happened

- No incident priority has been confirmed.
- No applicable SLA response target or clock position has been confirmed.
- Contract coverage for the upper-floor controls has not been confirmed.
- Commercial authority for chargeable diagnosis, parts or specialist attendance has not been confirmed.
- No engineer has accepted a dispatch.
- No technical diagnosis has been made.
- No customer commitment has been made beyond acknowledging the call.
