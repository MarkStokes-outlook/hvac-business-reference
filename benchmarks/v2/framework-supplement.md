# V2 methodological supplement

V2 inherits canonical authority, realistic complexity, credited uncertainty/inaction, declared critical failures and independent judging from `benchmarks/framework/benchmark-specification.md` and the frozen V1 framework. This supplement controls V2 only. It replaces the older application specification's 70/20/10 combined scoring and transcript-based assumption provenance with independently reported dimensions and observable semantic grounding. It does not alter that specification, V1's frozen scoring or any past result.

OF evidence is a V1 operational application run. V2 cannot retroactively claim V1 tested enforcement or new-user discovery. BR requires exercising the wrong path even when a competent user would avoid it. IU requires a user who has not learned the application's workflow. SF asks whether observable business meaning is grounded, without attributing the developer's thinking from unavailable transcripts. SI derives invariants from linked business records; exact-once transport, database isolation or internal architectural patterns are not policy. PT requirements are observable business consequences of confidentiality, evidence preservation, field work and continuity; security techniques and accessibility conditions are predeclared benchmark probes, not FrostLine legislation or an invented service-level commitment.

Controls form a descriptive ladder, not a technology mandate:

| Control | When sufficient / observable assertion |
|---|---|
| Guidance | Help discover or clarify a reasonable route; business-equivalent choices remain available. |
| Warning | A visible, specific risk/consequence before commitment; test whether proceeding leaves a truthful and controlled state. A dismissed ineffective warning fails required stronger controls. |
| Confirmation | Deliberate acknowledgement of a reversible or permitted consequence, recorded where material. Confirmation cannot create missing approval or legal/technical competence. |
| Scoped authority | Proceed only on traceable, valid evidence of the required actor, scope, limit and conditions. Supported manual reviewed approval can qualify; a notes box saying “approved” by an unauthorised actor cannot. |
| Hard guardrail | A state/action boundary requires a verified prerequisite. Equivalent controlled process can qualify if the ordinary role cannot bypass it by the tested supported path. |
| Prohibited transition | Canonical prohibition cannot be legitimised by clicking “confirm”, general manager override or relabelling. A different genuinely authorised business event is a different transition. |

Oracle `minimum_control` declares required semantics, not an ordinal bonus. Stronger control earns no extra points and can fail valid-continuation/usability when it unnecessarily prevents authorised work. Where canonical evidence permits judgment, credit guidance, warnings or confirmations that actually preserve the declared constraint. Where permission/evidence is absent, evaluate held, refused or escalated state and accountable continuation; blanket refusal is not full fitness.

R03/R04/R07/R08/R10/R12/R16 include explicit prohibited or prerequisite-sensitive consequences. Positive stage 2 trials ensure the app can also proceed on valid evidence, including bounded procurement, valid clock stops, completed catch-up maintenance, charge conversion and justified complaint closure. Repeated event, concurrency, stale data and restart tests assert coherent commercial/operational consequences, not a particular uniqueness key or storage schema. A duplicate raw inbox item can be benign if reconciled before it creates misleading or duplicate commitment.
