# Semantic fidelity and application hallucination

This dimension concerns observable product meaning, not whether a developer wrote hallucinated prose. An invented concept can manifest as a required schema field, status, configuration, automated rule, workflow, default, report denominator, promise or authority boundary. Observe its effect through normal exposed operation; source/transcript inspection is not needed and is outside this authoring task.

## Census and classification

Administrator/evaluator records a role-by-role census of navigation, creation/edit flows, statuses, user-visible configuration/defaults, dashboards, templates/reports/exports, communications, automations and supported handoffs. Declare scope and unavailable surfaces. Inventory all consequential concepts and claims, plus a sample of low-impact optional fields/utilities. Include supported meanings and required omissions as well as apparent inventions. Do not sample only the narrow callout workflow. S01 requires business identity/services, work lifecycle, authority/commercial rules, safety/environment, information boundaries and reporting coverage.

For each unique concept capture: surface/action; exact app claim or implied rule; source observation/artifact; persisted effect; applicable fixture/source; canonical path/heading/evidence ID; searched canonical scope if none; classification; confidence; materiality; affected role/work/population; critical predicate if applicable; evaluator disagreement. Keep raw effects separate from author's interpretation. Active probes are required for consequential defaults/automation: attempt omitted prerequisites, alternative scope/authority, unknown values, premature states or repeated events as appropriate in sandbox. Merely seeing a policy dropdown is insufficient.

| Class | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| Explicitly grounded | Canonical text establishes the actual business concept/meaning; behavior conforms, including relevant limits. |
| Correctly inferred | Not stated verbatim; reproducible chain from canonical constraints supports this meaning without a new enduring fact. State each inference and reasonable alternatives. |
| Benign implementation assumption | Technical/presentation convenience with no invented business obligation, entitlement, identity or decision authority, and no material misleading consequence. Example: stable internal identifier, reversible optional colour label. |
| Unsupported business invention | Presented/implemented as FrostLine business meaning but canonical scope search cannot establish it. Preserve evidence gap; do not claim contradiction simply from silence. |
| Contradictory invention | Actual asserted/implemented meaning conflicts with established authoritative evidence; identify exact constraint and demonstrated consequence. |
| Irrelevant capability presented as business meaning | Unnecessary/irrelevant functionality is marketed or required as meaningful FrostLine capability without grounded role/service purpose. Optional general export/search is not automatically irrelevant. |

An explicit synthetic agreement/delegation can ground the episode behavior, but never a company-wide default. “Correctly inferred” is an evidence argument, not permission to invent commonly used HVAC policy. A required serial field with no honest unknown/reconciliation route can create false completeness; a harmless optional unknown field does not. A generic department dropdown does not prove FrostLine has that department; a compulsory approval route through an invented head of IT does assert business meaning.

## Materiality

Bands are transparent evaluator conventions derived from observable business consequences, not a claim that `docs/` specifies these numbers:

| Band | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0 — benign | Optional/reversible presentation or technical assumption; no misleading business state or consequential restriction. |
| 1 — minor | Local wording/extra field or unnecessary low-impact step causing limited confusion, readily corrected without obligation/history change. |
| 2 — material | Changes scope, payer, eligibility, cost, scheduling, reporting, required work or accountable action; creates misleading state or blocks legitimate operation. |
| 3 — critical consequence | Actual safety/authority/confidentiality/commercial/evidence violation meeting an already declared critical predicate. |

Report count by class/band, maximum band, affected population and incidents, not just total inventions. One false free-repair policy affecting a portfolio is more material than twenty optional fields. Deduplicate the concept count; preserve every manifestation and action. Missing required semantics is reported separately and affects relevant scored assertion even with zero invention count. S01 critical gates are triggered only by demonstrated material commercial/authority/entitlement or contradictory safety/compliance/unsupported guaranteed-performance behavior, not a mere unevaluated screen.

Score S01 checkpoints using demonstrated constraints and coverage, not a formula of “number of fields minus hallucinations”. Materiality helps explain/gate the profile; no extra complexity earns points. Disputed classifications, inference chains, exhaustive scoped silence searches and materiality judgments are independently recorded and adjudicated against exact canonical evidence. True silence becomes an evidence gap. A source conflict makes the affected expectation unresolved rather than allowing the judge to manufacture policy.
