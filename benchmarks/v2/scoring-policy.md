# Dimensional scorecard and gates

Each additional checkpoint belongs to exactly one of BR, IU, SF, SI or PT and scores **2** (complete observed expected consequence), **1** (material correct subset, no material contradiction), **0** (contradicted, absent or not demonstrated). Explain the subset and missing element. Critical violation forces the associated checkpoint to 0 and remains explicit even if corrected. Missing observations/instrumentation mark unresolved; an attempted supported operation that the candidate cannot perform can legitimately score 0 with complete evidence.

Normalise within each **scenario × dimension** cell, then average those cells equally within that dimension. This prevents a longer rubric or a multi-domain narrow workflow from gaining weight. Report every cell, raw checkpoint decisions and per-dimension denominator/coverage. OF is reported exactly under V1's own scoring, pass/fail and critical policy, never normalised into a new average. No headline arithmetic average across six dimensions is valid.

For SF also report the inventory and materiality profile; the SF percentage cannot conceal a critical invention. Count unique business concepts (one invented policy shown in six surfaces is one concept with six observations), while separately reporting affected actions/population. Absence is missing required meaning, not necessarily a hallucination. Unsupported and contradictory inventions are separate categories. Benign implementation decisions and generic optional utilities incur no automatic penalty.

Standard bounded deployment threshold (predeclared evaluation convention):

- All required scenarios/phases/observations complete and valid, with independent scoring and resolved adjudication.
- Each new dimension at least **80%**.
- Every scenario/dimension cell at least **60%**: no domain failure can hide behind its dimension mean.
- **No declared critical failure** in any additional dimension; no unresolved material inventory finding.
- Comparable complete frozen V1 OF has its own demonstrated-operational-fitness verdict and no failed operation under V1 policy.

These thresholds are conservative benchmark conventions, not canonical operating policy, an empirically calibrated adoption prediction or full production/security certification. Do not adjust them after seeing a scored candidate. Narrower deployment proposals may cite specific measured cells with explicit exclusions and open risks, but cannot use the standard whole-business verdict.

Verdicts:

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Bounded fitness demonstrated | All six gates met in a calibrated, independently assessed standard profile. |
| Bounded pilot gates met; calibration pending | Complete valid independently judged run passes thresholds but release calibration is still pending. |
| Material fitness gaps demonstrated | Valid complete profile has critical event or fails a dimension/cell/OF operation. Report consequences and gates, regardless of other strengths. |
| Additional profile only | New dimensions valid but no comparable complete OF measurement. No whole-business deployment claim. |
| Incomplete or unresolved | Missing scenario/phase, invalid bundle, missing judges/adjudication, scope census incomplete or source/instrumentation defect. Preserve observed failures but do not declare overall fitness. |

No exemptions are inferred from missing features. A product lacking AI has no AI-specific requirement; T03 uses its actual untrusted-data boundary. A product lacking a transport integration is tested at its native consequential boundary. A decision-support-only product can be reported as that scoped profile, never compared as an executed operational application. Raw unavailable metrics remain “not assessed”. Correct clarification/refusal/inaction and controlled manual approval count when evidenced by effective supported workflow; operator caution/notes alone cannot satisfy BR.

Critical predicates are declared in oracle checkpoints. Require an actual issued instruction, disclosure, commitment, persisted false state or material evidence destruction with artifact and canonical references, rather than hypothetical possibility. Similar checkpoint predicates triggered by one action form one incident linked to all affected checkpoints; count incidents separately from predicates. No cascading deductions, averaging away safety disputes or newly invented critical failures. S01 can gate material inventions only under its declared commercial/safety predicates; unrelated harmless extras do not trigger them.

Compare development methodologies/models using frozen tasks, grants, build/configuration, baseline, devices, assistance and scoring conventions. Blind judges to method labels until submission; disclose generating/evaluator model relationships. Report six-dimensional profiles, critical incidents and uncertainty, administration effort, usability distribution and exclusions. Do not reward schema size, feature count, architecture, framework, model prestige, fashionable patterns, internal style or coverage percentage.
