# Sealed evidence contract

`templates/observation.json` is an interchange contract, not an application schema. One scenario bundle contains its exact supplied facts/event delivery, fixture-loaded pre-state, raw actions and response, role/identity, condition receipts, source-versus-candidate changes, fresh persisted state, emitted sandbox events, assistance/deviations and immutable artifacts. Include genuine blocked/refused outcomes. Screenshots without consequential state/issued-event evidence are insufficient where persistence matters.

The observation record pins run/scenario, build and configuration digests, suite/canonical fingerprint, operator subjects and business briefing, stages and required phases. Each observation has stable ID, stage, actor, origin (`fixture`, `operator`, `candidate`, `administrator`), kind, factual description and artifact references. Artifacts are sandbox UI captures/reports/exports/action traces/event sinks; no candidate development source/transcripts, old evaluation results or secrets. An asserted policy in notes must be labelled as assertion and cannot masquerade as candidate control. Record changed state separately from descriptions of intent.

`seal` computes artifact content SHA-256 and bundle fingerprint from canonical JSON serialization, writing outside repository. Paths must be relative safe regular files, never symlinks/traversal. `check-bundle` checks hashes, provenance, observation references, phase coverage and stage receipts. Complete receipts certify recorded administration, not successful application behavior: evaluator must independently confirm exact attempted actions/induced conditions from artifacts. Mechanical checks cannot establish truth from arbitrary screenshots or fabricated descriptions.

Judge records score, exact observed subset, canonical IDs, observation IDs, confidence, critical predicate decision and incident ID for every checkpoint. Scores of 1 need explicit missing element; unsupported required app operation has factual denial/limitation artifacts and can score 0. Missing/instrumentation uncertainty is `unresolved`, with no passing gate. Judges receive raw sealed artifacts plus hidden material and pinned canonical corpus, not another judge's scores, comparison methodology or author's private explanation.

`compare` rejects different runs/builds/bundles and same judge identity, reports every score/status/critical decision disagreement. Independent agreement still requires two recorded originals for finalisation. A final assessment preserves assessor IDs and fingerprints, adjudication artifact for disagreements, and all unresolved issues. Adjudication template retains both positions, observations/fixtures/canonical source, final reasoning and decision. Tools do not resolve judgment or average disagreements.

Semantic inventory retains census scope/completeness and each concept's six-way classification, materiality 0–3, observation/evidence refs, inference/gap search, consequences and unresolved status. Sealed inventory is an artifact in S01; missing/uncertain material concepts keep SF unresolved. Counts never replace effects.

Scorecard accepts one final assessment per scenario, rechecks corresponding sealed bundles and their artifact hashes, requires consistent suite/build/configuration/canonical provenance and recorded independent assessor originals/adjudication. It reports cells, dimensions, critical incidents and incompleteness; it cannot invent OF. Optional V1 attachment preserves exact frozen verdict, scores, provenance and result hashes. Original V1 run records are administration-time external evidence; tooling does not search for or author results.

The exporter returns an administrator-only `delivery_receipt` on stdout with initial fixture/task hashes or stage-event ID/payload fingerprint. Add its actual timestamp and observation references to `stage_deliveries`. Do not include suite/assessment metadata in user inputs. T03 supplementary CSV/text files are explicit dirty/untrusted episode source data; headers are interchange aids, not prescribed application schema.
