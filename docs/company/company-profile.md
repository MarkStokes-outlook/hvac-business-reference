# Company Profile

## Overview

Frostline Mechanical Services Ltd is a privately owned commercial HVAC contractor headquartered in Greater Manchester. The company provides design, installation, planned maintenance, reactive repair and replacement services for heating, ventilation, air-conditioning and refrigeration equipment.

Frostline operates primarily across Greater Manchester, Lancashire, Merseyside, Cheshire and West Yorkshire. Most work is completed within a two-hour travel radius of its main depot, although established customers occasionally ask the company to support sites elsewhere in England.

## Scale

Frostline employs 41 permanent members of staff and works regularly with a small pool of approved subcontract engineers and specialist trades. Its annual turnover is approximately £7.8 million, with revenue divided between installation projects, planned-maintenance contracts, reactive work and equipment replacement.

The company is large enough to run distinct sales, operations, service, installation, procurement, warehouse and finance functions, but small enough that senior managers remain closely involved in day-to-day delivery.

## Ownership

The business is owned by its two founders, Martin Hale and Susan Mercer, together with Operations Director Daniel Mercer, who acquired a minority shareholding in 2022. Martin serves as Managing Director. Susan is Finance Director. Daniel leads operational delivery.

The owners intend to remain independent. They are open to measured growth but do not want to become a national contractor or lose the company's reputation for practical, responsive service.

## Customers

Frostline works mainly with commercial and public-sector organisations, including:

- independent and regional retail groups;
- hotels, restaurants and leisure venues;
- schools, colleges and academy trusts;
- office landlords and managing agents;
- light-industrial and warehousing businesses;
- facilities-management providers;
- care providers and supported-living operators; and
- multi-site owner-operated businesses.

Some customers own a single building. Others operate dozens of sites under one commercial relationship. The organisation paying for work is not always the organisation occupying the site or owning the equipment.

## Services

Frostline's principal services are:

- commercial HVAC design and installation;
- equipment replacement and system upgrades;
- planned preventive maintenance;
- statutory and manufacturer-recommended servicing;
- reactive fault diagnosis and repair;
- out-of-hours emergency response for contracted customers;
- commissioning and handover;
- warranty investigation and remedial work; and
- supply and installation of replacement parts and controls.

The company does not manufacture equipment. It installs and supports systems from several established manufacturers and uses specialist partners for work such as major electrical alterations, scaffolding, lifting operations and some controls integration.

## Position in the market

Frostline competes on technical competence, responsiveness and continuity of service rather than lowest price. It is often selected by customers who have outgrown a sole trader but do not want the rigidity or account-management overhead of a national facilities contractor.

Its strongest customer relationships combine installation work with ongoing maintenance. The company values recurring service revenue because it is more predictable than project work and creates long-term knowledge of customer sites and installed equipment.

## Current stage of development

Frostline has grown steadily rather than explosively. Practices that worked when the founders personally knew every customer and engineer are now under strain as the number of employees, sites, assets, contracts and concurrent jobs has increased.

The business remains profitable and well regarded. It is not in crisis. Its challenge is that coordination increasingly depends on experienced people, local spreadsheets, inboxes, phone calls and individual memory. Those practices still work often enough to survive, but no longer scale comfortably.
