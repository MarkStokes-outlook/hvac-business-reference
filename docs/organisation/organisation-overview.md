# Organisation Overview

## Leadership

Frostline is led by three working directors:

- **Martin Hale — Managing Director**  
  Responsible for overall direction, major customer relationships, commercial decisions and business development. Martin remains involved in significant quotations and sensitive customer issues.

- **Susan Mercer — Finance Director**  
  Responsible for finance, payroll, credit control, insurance, commercial administration and supplier accounts. Susan also oversees office administration and remains a key source of historical company knowledge.

- **Daniel Mercer — Operations Director**  
  Responsible for installation delivery, service operations, engineering standards, resource planning and operational performance.

The directors meet formally each month, but many decisions are still made through frequent informal conversations.

## Functional structure

Frostline has seven main functional areas.

### Sales and estimating

A Commercial Manager, two estimators and one sales coordinator handle enquiries, surveys, quotations, tender responses and commercial follow-up. Martin supports strategic opportunities and large project bids.

### Installation operations

An Installation Manager coordinates project engineers, installation engineers, apprentices and external trades. The team delivers new systems, replacements, upgrades and commissioning work. Project engineers lead individual installation projects and are referred to in other contexts as project managers when performing that coordination role; "project manager" is a function rather than a separate job title at Frostline's current scale.

### Service operations

A Service Manager leads planned maintenance, reactive work and warranty response. Two service coordinators manage incoming calls, engineer diaries, customer updates and follow-on visits.

### Field engineering

Frostline employs installation engineers, service engineers and apprentices. It also uses a small pool of approved subcontract engineers when specialist skills or additional capacity are required. Engineers spend most of their working time away from the depot.

### Procurement and warehouse

A Procurement and Stores Manager, supported by a warehouse assistant, handles supplier orders, deliveries, stock, project materials, van stock and returns.

### Finance and administration

The finance team manages purchase invoices, sales invoicing, job costing, payroll support, expenses, credit control and general administration.

### Compliance and people

Compliance responsibilities are shared between the Operations Director, managers and an Office and Compliance Coordinator. The company does not currently have separate HR, legal, IT or information-security departments. External specialists are used when needed.

## Organisational character

Frostline is structured enough to have clear departments, but work frequently crosses those boundaries. A service visit may identify a replacement opportunity, an installation project may create a long-term maintenance contract, and a warranty issue may require input from sales, engineering, procurement and finance.

This cross-functional character is commercially valuable but creates coordination pressure. No single department has a complete view of every customer, site, asset, quote, job, contract and financial consequence.

## Decision-making

Routine decisions are delegated to managers, but exceptions often escalate to one of the directors. Staff value the directors' accessibility and practical judgement. The downside is that unusual cases can become dependent on whichever experienced person remembers the customer, contract or previous agreement.

Frostline's management style is pragmatic rather than procedural. Written policies exist for safety, compliance and core financial controls, while many day-to-day operating decisions rely on established practice and manager discretion.
