# Services Overview

Frostline sells engineering outcomes rather than a single standard product. The same customer may engage the company for a one-off installation, an ongoing maintenance contract, emergency support and later equipment replacement. This document uses the record meanings in [Canonical terminology and record relationships](../governance/canonical-terminology-and-record-relationships.md).

## Commercial HVAC installation

Frostline designs and installs air-conditioning, ventilation, heat-pump and associated control systems for commercial premises. Typical projects range from small office refurbishments to multi-zone systems across retail, hospitality, education and light-industrial sites.

Installation work may include survey, design coordination, equipment selection, quotation, procurement, programme planning, site installation, testing, commissioning, documentation and customer handover.

Frostline normally acts as a specialist contractor rather than principal contractor. On larger projects it works under a main contractor or mechanical-and-electrical contractor and must align with wider site programmes, access rules and construction documentation.

## Replacement and upgrade work

Existing systems are replaced because of age, reliability, refrigerant availability, energy performance, changed building use or repeated repair cost. Replacement opportunities may arise from surveys, maintenance visits, breakdowns or customer planning.

Some replacement work is straightforward. Other work requires temporary cooling or heating, phased installation, out-of-hours access, crane lifts, electrical upgrades or coordination with landlords and occupants.

## Planned maintenance

Maintenance contracts provide scheduled inspection and servicing of customer equipment. Visit frequency and scope vary by equipment, customer need, manufacturer guidance and contract.

A maintenance visit may include cleaning, operational checks, measurements, minor adjustments, filter replacement, refrigerant checks, visual inspection and identification of emerging faults. Engineers record findings and may recommend remedial work.

Maintenance contracts range from a handful of assets at one site to portfolios covering many sites and several hundred items of equipment.

## Reactive repair

Customers report faults by phone, email or through facilities-management channels. Frostline assesses urgency, contract cover, site constraints and likely skill requirements before arranging attendance.

The first attendance may resolve the fault, identify a need for parts, recommend replacement, or conclude that another trade or manufacturer is required. Reactive work often creates follow-on activity rather than resolving the customer issue in a single attendance.

## Emergency support

Selected contracted customers receive out-of-hours support for business-critical failures. Availability and response expectations depend on the contract; Frostline does not promise the same service to every customer.

The on-call engineer may provide telephone advice, attend site, make a temporary repair or make equipment safe pending a full response. Emergency work commonly requires later review, parts ordering, customer approval and a return visit.

## Commissioning and handover

New and replacement systems are tested, commissioned and handed over with the records appropriate to the work. Depending on project scope, this may include commissioning results, equipment schedules, operating information, warranties, certificates, photographs and customer training.

The company sometimes commissions systems installed by others where it has accepted a clearly defined scope and can verify the installation sufficiently.

## Warranty support

Frostline investigates faults reported during workmanship or manufacturer warranty periods. Warranty status is not always straightforward: equipment, parts and labour may have different cover, and eligibility may depend on commissioning, registration, servicing or manufacturer approval.

An attendance initially treated as warranty work may later prove chargeable, while a chargeable call-out may identify a valid manufacturer claim. Commercial treatment is confirmed once the cause and applicable cover are understood.

## Parts and controls

Frostline supplies and fits replacement components, controls and ancillary equipment as part of installation or service work. It does not operate as a general parts retailer, although established customers occasionally purchase specific items through the company.

## Specialist partners

The company routinely coordinates with Frostline-approved subcontractors and specialist partners for scaffolding, access equipment, lifting, specialist electrical work, builders' work, water treatment, controls integration and other services outside Frostline's direct capability.

Frostline remains responsible to its customer for the work it has contracted to deliver, even where parts are performed by others.
